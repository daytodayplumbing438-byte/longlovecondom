Who should stick with a regular condom

  • Anyone with a known sensitivity to benzocaine or similar anesthetics (the "-caine" family).
  • Men who find any reduction in sensation makes it hard to stay hard. Try one first before buying a big pack.

Does it feel different?

Putting it on feels the same as any lubricated latex condom. The first thing people notice, a few minutes in, is a slight cooling or "quieter" sensation at the tip. After that most say it simply feels like sex that lasts longer. It does not feel like a thick or heavy condom, because it isn't one; the delay comes from the lubricant, not the latex.
